This is AI agents A to Z series — Blog #6

In the previous blog How to choose the right model for your AI agent?, we explored how to make the right choice for your AI model. In this one we would understand how orchestration works to enhance your AI agent capabilities manifold.

On a high level there are 2 types of AI agent architectures

  1. Single- Agent Architectures
  2. Multi-Agent Architectures

Single-Agent Architectures

A single-agent architecture is what the name actually suggests: a self-contained system with one autonomous unit.

  • It consists of a single agent responsible for doing all the work.
  • It has its own AI model (the brain), its memory/context management (to remember past interactions), and a set of Tools (like a calculator or a web-browser) to interact with the world and gather data.
